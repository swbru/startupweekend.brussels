## 🌟 About Startup Weekend Brussels

Startup Weekend Brussels is part of the global Startup Weekend movement that has:

- 🌍 Reached **3,000+ cities** worldwide
- 👥 Trained **1M+ entrepreneurs**
- 🚀 Launched **25,000+ startups**
- 💰 Helped raise **$100M+** in funding

### What is Startup Weekend?

A **54-hour event** where entrepreneurs, developers, designers, marketers, and startup enthusiasts come together to:

1. **Friday Evening**: Pitch ideas in 60 seconds and form teams
2. **Saturday**: Build, develop, and validate your startup
3. **Sunday**: Present to judges and compete for prizes
